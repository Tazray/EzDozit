# Privacy Statement

Ez Dozit is built on a simple promise: **your data is yours, and it stays on your phone.**

## What We Collect

Nothing. Ez Dozit has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no telemetry. We don't know who you are, where you go, or how you use the app.

## Where Your Data Lives

All data is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. This includes:
- Your explored map tiles
- Your Haven locations and notes
- Your adventure session history
- Your encounter counts
- Your daily reflections

None of this data is ever transmitted off your device. There is no cloud sync, no backup service, and no "anonymous" usage data.

## Permissions and Why We Need Them

| Permission | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| Location (fine) | To show your position on the map and reveal explored areas |
| Location (background) | To continue tracking during active sessions when the screen is off |
| Bluetooth scan | To detect nearby people for the encounter system |
| Bluetooth connect | To identify your own paired devices (so we can ignore them) |
| Activity recognition | For optional step counting during adventures |
| Foreground service | To keep the adventure session running reliably |
| Notifications | To show the "Ez is exploring with you" notification during sessions |

Every permission is requested contextually — only when you first use the feature that needs it, with a clear explanation of why.

## What We Will Never Do

- Send your location to any server
- Track you when you're not actively using the app
- Store actual Bluetooth MAC addresses (we use daily-rotating hashes)
- Include advertising or tracking SDKs
- Sell, share, or transmit any data about you
- Add analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry that phones home

## Open Source Guarantee

Ez Dozit is open source under AGPL-3.0. You can read every line of code to verify these claims. If we ever break these promises, you'll know — and you can fork the project.

## Data Export and Deletion

You own your data. We will provide tools to:
- Export all your data in a standard format
- Delete all app data completely

Uninstalling the app removes all data from your device.
